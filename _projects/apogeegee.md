We returned to Timmins at Launch Canada 2024 with our minimum diameter rocket, *Apogee-Gee*, aiming towards the O-class motor altitude record.
Learning from our experience with Gee-Force One, the team has decided to significantly redesign the arming systems and deployment charge connectors.
The avionics section was also redesigned upon HYDRA V2, with quality-of-life upgrades and a radical size reduction.
This was also our first year using our SRAD Antenna Tracker, which was in development and manually operated.

At the launch, HYDRA faced multiple difficulties and was unable to be incorporated to *Apogee-Gee* before launch.
One of our COTS flight computer, Aim Xtra, was also damaged during testing, leading to a switch to a Blue Raven Altimeter and incorporating it into our avionics system.
In flight, *Apogee-Gee* was also affected by the exterior paint and resulted in a much lower apogee.
Overall, *Apogee-Gee* had a nominal flight in 2024 and achieved 9th place in the Basic Launch Category.

    <p>2025</p>
</hgroup>

The story of *Apogee-Gee* was not over just yet. We brought it back with us to Launch Canada 2025!
While most of the airframe and recovery systems remain the same, we have reduced the size of the main parachute, stripped the rocket of any decorative layer and modified the avionics bay to fit our new flight computer, PHOENIX.

Despite our efforts to reattempt at the M-class motor altitude record, *Apogee-Gee* suffered from a series of obstacles, from issues with our batteries, to defective motor adhesive.
Ultimately, *Apogee-Gee* went up into the clouds and experienced a rapid unscheduled disassembly.

Even though the results were not ideal, we remained determined to recover as much as possible.
Thanks to our telemetry data and visual sightings from fellow competitors, volunteers and spectators, we were able to recover the nose cone, the avionics bay, a broken half of a fin and our torn drogue parachute.
The second flight of *Apogee-Gee* was devastating to the team, but it was an important experience for us to improve our documentation and procedural skills to have as much control over the variables as we can.
While *Apogee-Gee* lies in the forest of Timmins, its spirit as a minimum diameter rocket lives on as [Perigee-Gee](/projects/perigeegee).
