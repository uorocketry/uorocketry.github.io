*Simple Latte* was uORocketry’s first rocket. After two years of development on the hybrid engine, the team decided to pivot towards a competitive goal, the Spaceport America Cup 2018 Intercollegiate Rocket Engineering Competition.

<!-- TODO: tables -->

At Spaceport America Cup 2018, *Simple Latte* came in ballistic and almost all of the rocket is lost in the white sands desert of New Mexico. Thankfully, our nose cone was found by another team and remained with the team for testing purposes. *Simple Latte* will forever be in our memory.
