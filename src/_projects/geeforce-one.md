At Launch Canada 2023, we launched our tallest rocket yet, *Gee-Force One*. Some highlights of this rocket are HYDRA, the advanced SRAD avionics system featuring custom PCBs; and our brand-new telemetry ground station.

At the launch, our main parachute unfortunately deployed at apogee due to a miswiring issue with our COTS flight computer. Despite this, the overall flight of *Gee-Force One* was successful!

Not only was our maximum velocity (Mach 1.76) extremely close to our predicted value (Mach 1.84), but our SRAD avionics system HYDRA also performed as intended, logging more than 6 million data points! Another major success was the collection of footage during the flight, which was extremely helpful in post-flight inspections and analysis. We were even awarded 5th place in the Basic Launch Category!

*Gee-Force One* is currently on display in the John McEntyre Team Space in the STEM Complex (STM).
