A collaborative effort between all our sub-teams. We aim to launch a rocket that incorporates the designs of all of our SRAD components at LC27!

To ensure our manufacturing, documentation and procedural skills meet the standards to accomplish such ambitious project, each sub-team has dedicated the 2026-2027 year not just to design for LC26 but also lay the foundation for the years to come.
