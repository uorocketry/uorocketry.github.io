At Launch Canada 2026, we launched *Perigee-Gee*, an even smaller, slimmer minimum diameter rocket compared to [Apogee-Gee](/projects/apogeegee). Instead of aiming for the O-class motor altitude record, we aimed for the M-class motor altitude record.

One major characteristic with *Perigee-Gee* is sacrificing many previous designs to ensure it goes higher and faster. These sacrifices include the CO2 deployment devices and our SRAD avionics flight computer (PHOENIX). The fins were also switched from composites to aluminum to avoid problems encountered by Apogee-Gee.
