An engineer knows how to design and manufacture a part.
That’s what we do!!
Airframe is responsible for designing and manufacturing the internal rocket structure.
This sub-team also runs simulations and analyzes structural parts.
If you are interested in mechanical design, this sub-team is the one!!

On the Airframe team, you could be working on:
- Designing the internal structures of the rocket
- Running different simulations (Structural, Topology, Thermal, etc.) using SOLIDWORKS to ensure the designs work!
- Designing tubes and nose cones using composite analysis
- Working with other sub-teams to make sure they fit inside the rocket
- Machining parts and fabricating the rocket
- Contacting vendors to ensure the proper manufacturing of different parts
