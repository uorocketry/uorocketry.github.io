A spin-off project from our Avionics team.
Beamforming is aiming to compete in the 2027 Launch Canada Challenge in the Technology Development Category.
This project utilizes many antennas that are phase-shifted from each other to concrete power towards an intended target in 3D space, essentially tracking our rocket.

Beamforming is structured in a 4-stage plan:
-   Phase 1 (Summer 2025 – Winter 2025)
    * Grasp and master fundamentals of beamforming/RF signal formation with COTS Radio SDR learning devices hands-on.
-	Phase 2 (Fall 2025 – Winter 2026)
    * Develop and validate two RF chain prototypes to verify the architecture's functionality and effectiveness.
-	Phase 3 (Winter 2026 – Fall 2026)
    * Develop and validate a smaller version of the board that will encompass the final antenna array, demonstrating prototype’s ability to scale into a final product.
-	Phase 4 (Fall 2026 – Summer 2027)
    * Develop final antenna array and custom radio module to be shown at LC27.
