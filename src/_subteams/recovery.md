Launching a rocket isn't our only goal.
We also need to get it back in one piece.
Recovery is responsible for returning our rocket safely to the ground.
This sub-team designs and builds parachutes, pyrotechnic fasteners, and parachute ejection systems.
Recovery also designs testing rigs and performs tests of our unique inverted-reefed parachute and its deployment systems.

On the Recovery team, you could be working on:
- Designing parachute deployment mechanisms
- Designing and sewing novel parachutes
- Designing and building test equipment
- Leading/participating in many, many tests
