# FAQs

## How can I join the team?
By filling out this [form](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=sdof1BV-_Uy1-nIA5U3ra85kgIk8EaVPsyEHt_kmPfdURVhUU0Q4UDNTSVRSSDMwWEhUNVBUODdJRiQlQCN0PWcu)!
In the beginning of every semester, we will go through the responses and let you know when and where to meet ASAP!

## Are there any requirements to join the team?
Not at all! While most of our members are in mechanical, electrical and software engineering, we’ve had students from many other programs be a part of our team, civil engineering, chemical engineering, computer science, nursing and even students from Telfer!

As long as you have a passion for rocketry, this team is the place for you.

## What if I don’t know anything about rocketry?
We can’t become rocket scientists in a day.
But with [Rocket Hacks](outreach#rocket-hacks) and sub-team workshops over the year, you’ll learn more as you go.
It’s a steep learning curve for sure, but you won’t be doing this alone.

## I’m not a uOttawa student yet, can I be a part of the team?
Over the years, we’ve had a few high school Co-Op and volunteer students participate in our team activities and our engineering process.
We evaluate these on a case-by-case basis. Please contact us at [rocketry@uottawa.ca](mailto:rocketry@uottawa.ca) for more information!

In addition, we also hold booths in the STEM Complex at Open Houses.

## What is the time commitment?
In general, you will spend at least 5 hours a week on team-related work and meetings.
If you’re part of an ongoing project like the liquid engine or design work, you may put in more time.
Regardless, every Saturday, each sub-team will meet and discuss our project progress.

So how much? Depends! At a certain point, you begin to lose track of how much time you’re putting into your passion project.

## What communication platform do you use?
We use [Slack](https://slack.com/)!
At our General Meetings (or other occasions), you will receive an invitation link to join our channels.
Just be sure to add the channels that you’re interested in!
