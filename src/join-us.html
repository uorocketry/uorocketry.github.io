Join Us

Do you want to be a member of the uORocketry team? If yes, you are at the right place! The uORocketry team is actively recruiting people to work on our exciting projects. Before completing the form to join us, we highly encourage you to read the description of each sub-team to have a better idea of what we do and what you could work on.

In addition to the six sub-teams, uORocketry has three sub-teams that are currently dormant: payload, flight dynamics and antenna tracker.

Payload has made its mark during the pre-COVID era, where we had developed a blood centrifuge experiment and an E. coli experiment. The former was incorporated in Jackalope, though was not permitted to fly due to its nature as a mammal-derived payload. The E. coli experiment began as a joint project with the Faculty of Biology and as part of the scrapped Rocktopus and later repurposed as our entry to CAN-RGX. If you have an idea, we are always excited to bring back Payload!

Flight Dynamics has been folded into Airframe.

Antenna Tracker is temporarily put on hold for LC26 and LC27, as most of the team’s personnel went into developing the liquid engine rocket, a new avionics architecture and the Beamforming project. However, we hope to one day continue development on our antenna tracker. The infrastructure is already here, we’re just looking for someone to perfect it! For now, Antenna Tracker has been folded into Airframe.
