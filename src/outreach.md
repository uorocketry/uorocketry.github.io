# Activities & Outreach

## Rocket Hacks
Every September, we organize a miniature rocket competition for new members!
At Rocket Hacks, you’ll be given a motor, cardboard, paper and many other materials to build a mini rocket.

Not only is this a fun activity to start your school year, but also an enriching activity for you to learn the basics of rocketry and meet people from your program.

![Team members preparing a rocket for launch at Rocket Hacks 2024](assets/Images/Activities/2024_RocketHacks.jpg)

How do you win, you might ask? If your rocket reaches the highest altitude and/or reaches the longest flight time, you’ll get a prize from the team!

Stay tuned to our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/uorocketry/) for more information!

## Outreach
Outreach and education are also crucial to our responsibilities as part of the uOttawa [Centre for Entrepreneurship and Engineering Design (CEED)](https://www.uottawa.ca/faculty-engineering/centre-entrepreneurship-engineering-design).
We regularly provide tours to interested visitors and members of our shared workspace, which is utilized by many other teams.
Additionally, we actively promote our work in the hopes of engaging more students and expanding our team's capabilities!
