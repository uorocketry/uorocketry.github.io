Sponsors

We are a student run organization that runs almost exclusively through the generous support of our sponsors. We simply would not fly without these contributions. Your contributions are helping develop the future of the Canadian space industry and the development of exciting medical research. This fosters a creative, inventive environment where students are able to make and improve on their mistakes. Beyond that, your organization becomes part of our team. With ample opportunities to visit, frequent featuring on our social media platforms, and flying your logo on a sounding rocket, we make sure to further your brand.

Swagelok is a global company that specialises in pressure and fluid system components. They have graciously supplied us with tube, fittings and valves for all our propulsion needs.

Gosco Valves and Kinetrol specialises in valve manufacturing and actuator solutions. Just this past year, they have kindly supplied us with cryogenic valves for our liquid engine project, Incendium Terrae.

SOLIDWORKS is a software company that specializes in 3D design and solutions. From modelling, to simulations, to calculations, SolidWorks is extremely useful to the team in every way imaginable.

Altium is a crucial software in our printed circuit board designs. This year, Altium has generously provided our avionics team with licenses for our flight computer development and other avionics projects.

Ansys is a multinational company that focuses on the development of engineering simulations. For the past few years, Ansys has graciously provided our team with licenses for our beamforming project.

As the team’s avionics division moves entirely to C for the 2026 – 2027 school year, IAR has kindly provided our teams with IAR Embedded Workbench licenses to help us catch issues early and maintain a more reliable codebase.

MetalPros is an Ottawa-based metal shop that has been long supported our team and many others in the John McEntyre Team Space. Whenever we need metal, MetalPros has always answered the call!

HAKKO is a manufacturing company of soldering and desoldering equipment. They have previously provided us with high-quality soldering irons and a hot air rework station.

LabJack has long supported our team’s engine development, most recently with data acquisition system equipment for Incendium Terrae.

Kulite has graciously provided us with transducers to help us improve the design of the combustion chamber and ensure the safety of our propulsion team members.
