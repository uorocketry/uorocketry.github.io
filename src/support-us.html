Support Us

Technical Sponsorship

We’re always looking to see what new interesting technologies we can use for our mission! Partnering with uORocketry allows for companies to test how their products apply in the realm of rocketry on a small scale. Without the valuable donation of materials, components and expertise from industry sponsors, our team wouldn’t be on the leading edge of performance. If you are interested in our team, please feel free to reach out to us about working together!

Monetary Sponsorship

To achieve our goals, we need your help. Your contribution will directly support our goals of being out of this world. Your donation helps create a collaborative environment where students are able to apply their studies out of the lecture hall, meet, and compete against students from around the globe!
